A made-to-measure glass floor replacement

Following a cracked floor tile at one of our Central London sites, Red Balloon Construction coordinated with specialist partners to produce a new walk-on glass tile with a matching geometric sandblasted finish.

The existing tile was carefully removed using specialist suction-lifting equipment before the new tile was installed. Weighing over 300kg, the replacement required careful planning, coordination and handling throughout the operation.
